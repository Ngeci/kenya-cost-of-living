04 / WHAT THE DATA TELLS US

Key findings

Interpreting the numbers beyond the charts.

01

Food remained a major pressure point.

Food and non-alcoholic beverages recorded annual inflation of 4.8%, higher than headline inflation of 3.0%.

02

Transport price growth was modest.

Transport recorded annual inflation of 0.2% in December 2024, indicating relatively little year-on-year movement in its overall price index.

03

Overall prices continued to rise.

The overall CPI increased from 137.55 in December 2023 to 141.66 in December 2024.

05 / RESEARCH NOTES

About this analysis

This project explores Kenya's Consumer Price Index and inflation trends using published national statistics.

The Consumer Price Index measures changes in the price of a representative basket of goods and services consumed by households.
